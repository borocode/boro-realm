# 🌲 The Wild Frontier: Survival Guide

> *"Beyond the sanctuary of the peaks lies an untamed expanse where civilizations rise and legends are forged."*

---

## 🗺️ What is the Wild Frontier?
The **Wild Frontier** is the vast, procedural survival wilderness of Boro Realm starting `750` blocks away from the Alpine Sovereign Estate and extending infinitely across all cardinal directions. In the Frontier, Minecraft survival mode is fully enabled:
- **Full Terrain Modification:** Chop trees, mine ores, terraform landscapes, and construct bases.
- **Classic Survival Mechanics:** Hunger, mob spawning, crafting, enchanting, brewing, and nether exploration.
- **Resource Gathering:** Diamond mining, redstone machinery, crop farming, and livestock breeding.

---

## 🚀 How to Enter the Frontier
1. Complete **The Boro Gauntlet** upon first joining the server (or type `!gauntlet`).
2. Step upon the **Altar of the Shell** (`-201, 183, -143`) to complete your verification trial.
3. You will automatically receive the **Starter Kit** and be launched into the Frontier.
4. If you ever visit the Estate (`!estate`), simply type `!wild` in chat to be launched back into the survival wilderness!

## 🏰 Base Construction & Etiquette
- **Choosing Your Settlement:** We recommend building at least 1,000 blocks away from the center to ensure you have ample untouched territory for quarries, farms, and mega-builds.
- **Waypoints & Navigation:** Note your base coordinates (`F3` on Java, or enable Coordinates in Bedrock settings). You can return to the Estate at any time with `!estate` to trade for enchanted books, emeralds, and tools, then return to your base coordinates.
- **Community Respect:** Do not build within 150 blocks of another wanderer's settlement without their explicit consent.
- **Grief-Prevention & Rollbacks:** All block placements, chest accesses, and breakages in the Frontier are continuously indexed by **CoreProtect**. Griefing or stealing from active player settlements is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate rollback and ban.

---

## 💎 Economy & Trading
The 63 immortal villagers at the Alpine Sovereign Estate offer master-tier trades:
- **Librarians:** Mending, Unbreaking III, Fortune III, Efficiency V, Silk Touch, Protection IV, and Sharpness V.
- **Armorsmiths & Weaponsmiths:** Diamond armor, swords, axes, and shields.
- **Toolsmiths:** Diamond pickaxes, shovels, and hoes.
- **Fletchers & Farmers:** Emerald conversion for sticks, wheat, carrots, potatoes, and pumpkins.

Bring your resources back to the Estate via `!estate` to exchange raw materials for emeralds and legendary enchantments!

## 🏔️ Overview
The **Alpine Sovereign Estate** is the architectural heart and communal sanctuary of Boro Realm. Situated on the rugged peaks centered around coordinates `[-283, 80, -356]`, the Estate represents **50 continuous building phases** and cohesive architectural craftsmanship designed under strict Classical Alpine Feng Shui principles.

## 🌿 Feng Shui Architectural Principles
1. **Backing the Mountain (玄武 - Black Tortoise):** The Sovereign Grand Chalet is anchored against the towering northern granite cliffs, providing shelter against winds and projecting solid security.
2. **Facing the Water (朱雀 - Red Phoenix):** The southern and eastern facades open outward across Reflection Lake and the lower river valley, inviting light and open mountain vistas.
3. **Flanking Sentinels (青龍 / 白虎 - Azure Dragon & White Tiger):** The eastern Mountain Gate Ramparts and western Lookout Ridge frame the central terrace with balanced defensive elegance.
4. **Flowing Qi (氣):** Unbroken, 3-block-wide paved stone-brick and timber promenades link every terrace, landing, dock, and summit without dead ends or jarring elevation changes.
